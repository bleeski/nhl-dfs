## Decisions on the follow-up review (A)

| # | Element | Decision | Reasoning | Lands in |

| A1 | Field sampler reuses a distinct-candidates generator, so it cannot produce the duplicates it must predict | **Accept** | Correct. Opponent entries are draws with replacement; distinctness is a property of one entrant's portfolio. The sampler now records multiplicities and the duplicate estimate is the weighted count. | Plan §6; C2a (`distinct` flag), C3 |
| A2 | Baseline places a network call before local generation; one call can take ~22 s; solver-free fallback not explicitly wired; `find_one` cannot distinguish timeout from infeasibility | **Accept** | All four are real. Baseline is now local-only through publish; network enhancement follows as a bounded second pass. `find_one` returns FOUND, TIMEOUT, or INFEASIBLE_PROVEN. | A3 | Copy to the public path is not atomic; lock is per run while runs share a slate output; referee reuses the parsed pool and `check_lineup` and checks entry identity only with a parent | **Accept** | Public replacement is now same-directory temp plus `os.replace`, under a slate-level lock. The referee has its own CSV reader and its own rules implementation, always binds the output bytes to the input salary and entries hashes, and a property test checks the two rule implementations agree. | Plan §10; C0b, C2b |
| A4 | Participation, DK eligibility, actual lock, and the edit-stop buffer are conflated; unknown statuses treated as OUT; `isSwappable` treated as not playing | **Accept** | Four separate states with a recognized status map; unknown stays UNKNOWN; `isSwappable` is editability only; "started" means the clock passed the start, "edit-stop" is the engine's buffer. | A6 | "Highest-variance builds to WTA" is the wrong test; frontier monotonicity is not guaranteed; cash and satellite objectives unspecified | **Accept** | WTA allocation favors tie-adjusted first-place equity. | A7 | "≤40% of fees on one game" is infeasible for a single-game Showdown slate; exact payouts do not make modeled probabilities calibrated | **Accept** | Concentration limits are mode-aware: per-game cap applies only when the slate has more than one game; Showdown concentration is measured by Captain and shared failure scenario.
