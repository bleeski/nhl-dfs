## The question

One Showdown game is on record: ANA@VGK on 2026-10-02, 3 contests, 341 field lineups, 4 of them in the top 1%
(reviews/2026-10-03_standings_synthesis.md, Showdown section). A goalie was Captain in 23% of the field and in none of the 4 top-1% lineups;
the top-1% Captains were skaters 3 to 11% of the field captained (Gauthier, Luneau, Olofsson) plus Eichel; the field's chalk Captains Hart (a
goalie, -2.8 points) and Stone (1.3) busted, and both were ours. B49 proposes three changes: a Captain from the skaters unless the simulator
ranks the goalie's ceiling in the slate's top 3 (flag 12), a Captain leverage term, and a team split that follows the simulator's goal share
with the underdog's 5-1 as a sized sleeve. One game proves nothing. This chunk (1) adds the per-cohort Captain report that checks the
pattern on every saved Showdown slate, (2) builds the first two changes as switches that ship OFF and the third as a report, and (3) tests
the switches on the model's own figure. Acceptance waits on standings from the next five Showdown slates (the card).

## The switches, exact (config/risk.yaml `discovery.captain`; both ship `enabled: false`)

- **Ceiling (flag 96).** A person's ceiling is the 99th percentile of his simulated points over the design scenarios, one column per person
  (the Captain's 1.5x multiplies every draw and does not change the order). His rank is 1 + the number of players on the slate with a
  strictly higher ceiling, counted over skaters and goalies together, so a tie at 3rd is allowed.
- **Goalie gate (flag 96).** With `goalie_gate.enabled`, a goalie whose rank is above `top_k` (3) is barred as Captain: his Captain row is
  excluded from every scenario-pass discovery job (`LineupModel(exclude=...)`), and bank and provisional candidates with that Captain are
  dropped before selection. Skater Captain rows are never barred; FLEX rows are untouched. Every Showdown run reports each goalie's
  ceiling and rank and the would-be-barred goalies, switch on or off.
- **Leverage term (flag 97).** With `leverage.enabled`, every Captain row (skater and goalie) of every scenario-pass discovery job gains
  `weight x (ceiling / the slate's highest ceiling) x ln(100 / max(CPT own %, own_floor_pct))` points, weight 1.5, own_floor_pct 1.0. CPT
  own is the Captain role's ownership in the biggest contest's sampled field, the map C20's goalie term reads. For two top-ceiling players,
  30% owned against 5% is worth 2.7 points (about one noise standard deviation of a Captain row: perturb_sd 2.0 x 1.5); the largest spread is
  1.5 x (ln 100 - ln 2.5) = 5.5 points. Hand-set by arithmetic, labeled PRIOR, never fitted (C12).
- **Split report (flag 98).** Report only. A lineup leans to the team holding 4 or more of its 6 players (3-3 leans to none) and is a 5-1
  when that team holds 5. Goal share = lambda_T / (lambda_T + lambda_opp) from the simulator's fitted game rates. Target leans = the entries
  split by goal share (largest remainder); the underdog's 5-1 sleeve target = min(floor(0.10 x entries), the underdog's lean target). A pool
  of more than one game reads NOT_APPLICABLE.
- **Reach.** Only the scenario pass (v3). The baseline (v1), provisional (v2), late swap and QA re-solves are untouched. Selection, the
  Captain cap (`exposure.caps`, enforced in `portfolio.select`), the field model and FIELD_CALIBRATION are unchanged. With both switches off
  every Showdown and Classic pick is what it is today.

Deviations from the session prompt's defaults, named: the switches live in `risk.yaml` beside C20's goalie term, not in `ownership.yaml`
`field.captain_rules` (that block models the field's Captain picks); the leverage term carries B49's ceiling factor on top of the prompt's
log ownership; the term covers goalie Captains too (a skater-only term would penalize every goalie Captain and blur the two arms); the bed is
`tests/fixtures/mini/showdown`, not `late_swap/showdown2` (see Data).

## The report (scripts/standings_synthesis.py)

Per Showdown lineup it adds the Captain's CPT ownership in that contest (reconstructed from the lineups, as the script already does), the
team holding 4 or more of the 6 players and whether that team won (the goalie decision W, so a shootout counts). Per cohort (top 1%, top
10%, cash, rest, field) it adds the mean Captain CPT ownership, the share of Captains 10% CPT-owned or less, the share of leaning lineups
whose team won, and the split by team ("ANA 5-1"); the goalie-Captain share, the any-goalie share, the Captain position mix and the split
mix were already there. The pooled Showdown table gains the same keys and prints the pattern check below. Known limit: ownership is keyed by
normalized name, so two players with the same name on one slate merge.

## Arms, metric, seeds and settings

- **Arms:** O both off (the shipping tree), G gate only, L leverage only; G and L each decide against O. B both on, information only.
- **Metric:** per arm and seed, the mean modeled probability of a top-1% finish (`p_top1pct` in the manifest's `scenario.entries`) over the
  entries of families large_gpp, small_field and wta, measured on the REFEREE draws (the stream independent of selection). It is the model's
  own figure: a pass means the model rates the arm higher, never that it wins money.
- **Mechanism figures, deciding nothing except where the rule says so:** goalie-Captain entries, mean Captain ownership, distinct Captains,
  the largest Captain count against the cap, lineups changed against O at the same seed, goalie Captains the gate removed (a G arm that
  removes none is labeled "no goalie Captain removed", not "no effect": a smaller Captain count moves the cap's floor and the search), the
  modeled field's goalie-Captain share, RISK_BUDGET, and in O the mean referee P(top 1%) of goalie-Captain entries against skater-Captain
  entries (B49's simulator test).
- **Seeds:** 20261013 + i, i = 0 to 4, on the bed and on every real slate; every arm sees the same five worlds, so differences are paired.
- **Noise baseline FIRST:** O twice at seed 20261013 on identical inputs (B98). Reported (identical lineups or not, the metric both times);
  it is not a threshold. Runs are one at a time; any other python process at start and PYTHONHASHSEED are recorded.
- **Minimum detectable effect:** `2.78 x sd(O over the 5 seeds) / sqrt(5)` (2.78: the 95% two-sided t cutoff, 4 degrees of freedom).
- **Counts:** real slates at the shipping counts of `config/risk.yaml`; the bed at "medium" (design 1,000, selection 3,000, referee 3,000,
  field target 1,000), so bed and real figures are not comparable.

## Verdict, mechanical, in this order

Per switch (G or L) per slate, with `d_i` = arm minus O at seed i, `mean_d` their mean and `se_d = sd(d) / sqrt(5)`:
1. The slate failed to run, or has no entry of the three families: NO VERDICT.
2. MDE above 10% of O's mean: INCONCLUSIVE.
3. ACCEPT-TO-SHADOW only if ALL hold: `mean_d >= 2.78 x se_d`; `mean_d >= 0.03 x mean(O)`; at least 4 of 5 seeds have `d_i > 0`; RISK_BUDGET
   is not BREACHED in any arm seed where O's was not; and, for L only, the arm's goalie-Captain entries summed over the seeds do not exceed
   O's.
4. REJECT if `mean_d <= 0` or `mean_d < 0.01 x mean(O)`.
5. Anything else: INCONCLUSIVE.

Study verdict per switch:
- Any synthetic slate in the run: NOT MEASURED on real slates. The per-slate statuses are printed labeled synthetic and decide nothing.
- Fewer than 3 real slates with a verdict: NOT MEASURED.
- ACCEPT-TO-SHADOW if accepted on at least half the real slates and rejected on none. REJECT if rejected on at least half and accepted on
  none. Anything else: INCONCLUSIVE.

The thresholds (2.78, 3%, 1%, 10%, 4 of 5, half, 3 slates) are judgment; no earlier measurement stands behind them. B is information only.

**Pattern check (information for flag 12, decides nothing):** pooled over the saved Showdown contests, once with every date and once without
2026-10-02 (the out-of-sample reading): the pattern HOLDS when the top 1% pools at least 20 lineups, its goalie-Captain share is at least 10
points under the field's, and its mean Captain CPT ownership is at least 5 points under the field's; NOT SHOWN otherwise; TOO FEW when the
top 1% pools fewer than 20 lineups. Thresholds are judgment.

## Caveats written before the numbers

- The metric is model-internal. The field model's Captain picks decide what the model calls leverage: if it under-captains goalies, both
  the leverage term and the metric reward goalie Captains the real field over-captains. The modeled field's goalie-Captain share is printed
  beside the one observed figure (23%) for that reason.
- The gate may rarely bind: a goalie's 99th percentile (a win with 40 saves and a shutout is about 41 points) rivals a star skater's, so a
  likely starter often ranks in the top 3. A gate that removes nothing reads INCONCLUSIVE or REJECT, and is not tuned to bind.
- Discovery's time budget is wall-clock (`runtime.yaml` candidates.time_limit_total_s), so a slow machine can find fewer candidates (B98).
- The bed is synthetic, priors-only and one game of 16 players; it says nothing about any real slate.
